# Protocol 04: Home-Based Newborn Care (HBNC) Guidelines

Disclaimer: Educational and operational decision support for trained frontline health workers. Protocols are simplified hackathon materials and are not for clinical use. This system does not replace clinical judgment or qualified medical care.

---

### HBNC-1.1: Institutional Delivery Home Visit Schedule
For babies born in health institutions (hospital, CHC, PHC), the ASHA must conduct 6 mandatory home visits:
- Visit 1: Day 3 of life.
- Visit 2: Day 7 of life.
- Visit 3: Day 14 of life.
- Visit 4: Day 21 of life.
- Visit 5: Day 28 of life.
- Visit 6: Day 42 of life (6 weeks).

### HBNC-1.2: Home Delivery Visit Schedule
For babies delivered at home, the ASHA must conduct 7 mandatory home visits (an extra Day 1 visit):
- Visit 1: Day 1 of life (within 24 hours of birth).
- Visit 2: Day 3 of life.
- Visit 3: Day 7 of life.
- Visit 4: Day 14 of life.
- Visit 5: Day 21 of life.
- Visit 6: Day 28 of life.
- Visit 7: Day 42 of life.

### HBNC-1.3: Immediate Care at Birth and Thermal Protection
- Immediate drying: Dry baby with clean warm towel within 5 seconds of birth; remove wet towel and wrap in second warm, dry cloth.
- Delayed cord clamping: Wait 1 to 3 minutes before cutting cord to boost infant iron stores.
- Skin-to-Skin contact: Place naked baby on mother's bare chest immediately after birth.
- Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC): For Low Birth Weight (LBW) babies weighing less than 2,500g. Provide continuous skin-to-skin contact for at least 1-2 hours per session, combined with frequent exclusive breastfeeding.
- Bathing guidelines: Delay bathing for at least 48 hours for normal weight babies; delay for 7 days or until baby reaches 2.5 kg for LBW babies to prevent fatal hypothermia.

### HBNC-1.4: Exclusive Breastfeeding Initiation and Counseling
- Early initiation: Initiate breastfeeding within 1 hour of birth for all babies.
- Colostrum: First yellow thick milk is rich in protective antibodies (first natural vaccine); never discard colostrum.
- Strictly no pre-lacteal feeds: No honey, ghutti, animal milk, plain water, or sugar water.
- Exclusive breastfeeding: Only breast milk (no water or other liquids/foods) for the first 6 months of life (180 days).
- Feed on demand: At least 8 to 12 times in 24 hours, day and night.

### HBNC-1.5: Cord Care and Eye Care
- Umbilical Cord: Keep the stump clean and dry. Leave open to air.
- Do NOT apply cow dung, ash, mustard oil, ghee, kajal, or talcum powder to the umbilical stump.
- Chlorhexidine 7.1% (delivering 4% free chlorhexidine) solution/gel should be applied once daily to the umbilical stump for the first 7 days if recommended by local health department.
- Eye care: Clean eyes from inner to outer canthus with boiled cooled cotton swab; do not apply surma or kajal.
