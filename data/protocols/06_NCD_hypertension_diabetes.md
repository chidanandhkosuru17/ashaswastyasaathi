# Protocol 06: Non-Communicable Diseases (NCD) Screening & Follow-Up

Disclaimer: Educational and operational decision support for trained frontline health workers. Protocols are simplified hackathon materials and are not for clinical use. This system does not replace clinical judgment or qualified medical care.

---

### NCD-1.1: Population-Based Screening (CBAC Checklist)
- Every adult aged 30 years and older must be screened annually using the Community-Based Assessment Checklist (CBAC).
- Screening covers hypertension, diabetes mellitus, oral cancer, breast cancer, and cervical cancer.
- Suspected individuals with high CBAC risk scores (>= 4) must be referred to the Primary Health Centre / Ayushman Arogya Mandir (Sub Health Centre).

### NCD-1.2: Hypertension Cut-offs and Follow-Up Protocols
Blood pressure measurement on seated individual after 5 minutes of rest:
- Normal: Systolic BP < 120 mmHg and Diastolic BP < 80 mmHg.
- Pre-hypertension: Systolic 120-139 mmHg and/or Diastolic 80-89 mmHg. Advise dietary changes, physical activity, and rescreen in 1 year.
- Stage 1 Hypertension: Systolic 140-159 mmHg and/or Diastolic 90-99 mmHg. Refer to Medical Officer for diagnosis and medication. Follow-up visit required every 30 days.
- Stage 2 Hypertension: Systolic >= 160 mmHg and/or Diastolic >= 100 mmHg. Urgent referral to Chandrapuri District Hospital / PHC.
- Hypertensive Emergency: Systolic >= 180 mmHg or Diastolic >= 110 mmHg with severe headache, chest pain, or visual disturbance -> Emergency referral immediately.

### NCD-1.3: Diabetes Mellitus Screening and Blood Sugar Cut-offs
Screening using point-of-care glucometer:
- Random Blood Sugar (RBS):
  - Normal: < 140 mg/dL.
  - Suspected Diabetes: 140 to 199 mg/dL. Refer to PHC for Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) test.
  - High / Diabetes Range: >= 200 mg/dL accompanied by classic symptoms (polydipsia, polyuria, unexplained weight loss). Refer for confirmation.
- Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS):
  - Normal: 70 to 99 mg/dL.
  - Impaired Fasting Glucose (Pre-diabetes): 100 to 125 mg/dL.
  - Diabetes: >= 126 mg/dL.
- Follow-up: All confirmed diabetic patients require monthly compliance visits and HbA1c testing every 3-6 months.

### NCD-1.4: Medication Adherence, Foot Care, and Lifestyle Counseling
- Medication Adherence: Reiterate that medicines for BP and diabetes must be taken every single day without interruption, even when feeling well.
- Salt Intake: Restrict dietary salt to less than 1 teaspoon (< 5 grams) per day. Avoid papad, pickles, and processed snacks.
- Physical Activity: At least 30 minutes of brisk walking or moderate physical activity 5 days a week.
- Tobacco and Alcohol Cessation: Total cessation of bidi, cigarettes, gutkha, khaini, and alcohol.
- Daily Foot Care for Diabetics: Inspect feet daily for cuts, blisters, redness, or fungal infection; wash and dry between toes; never walk barefoot.
