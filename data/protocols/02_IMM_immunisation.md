# Protocol 02: Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP) Guidelines

Disclaimer: Educational and operational decision support for trained frontline health workers. Protocols are simplified hackathon materials and are not for clinical use. This system does not replace clinical judgment or qualified medical care.

---

### IMM-1.1: Birth Doses (Within 24 Hours of Birth)
- BCG: 0.05 ml (if given <1 month age; 0.1 ml if given after 1 month) intradermal (ID) on left upper arm. Protects against childhood tuberculosis.
- OPV-0: 2 drops orally.
- Hepatitis B Birth Dose: 0.5 ml intramuscular (IM) on anterolateral aspect of mid-thigh. Must be administered within 24 hours of birth to prevent mother-to-child transmission.

### IMM-1.2: Six Weeks Immunisation
- OPV-1: 2 drops orally.
- Pentavalent-1 (DPT + Hep B + Hib): 0.5 ml IM, anterolateral aspect of mid-thigh.
- Rotavirus-1 (RVV): 5 drops orally (or 2.5 ml depending on presentation). Protects against rotavirus diarrhoea.
- fIPV-1 (Fractional Inactivated Polio Vaccine): 0.1 ml ID on right upper arm.
- PCV-1 (Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine): 0.5 ml IM on anterolateral mid-thigh.

### IMM-1.3: Ten Weeks Immunisation
- OPV-2: 2 drops orally.
- Pentavalent-2: 0.5 ml IM on anterolateral mid-thigh.
- Rotavirus-2: 5 drops orally.

### IMM-1.4: Fourteen Weeks Immunisation
- OPV-3: 2 drops orally.
- Pentavalent-3: 0.5 ml IM on anterolateral mid-thigh.
- Rotavirus-3: 5 drops orally.
- fIPV-2: 0.1 ml ID on right upper arm.
- PCV-2: 0.5 ml IM on anterolateral mid-thigh.

### IMM-1.5: Nine to Twelve Months Immunisation
- Measles-Rubella (MR) 1st Dose: 0.5 ml subcutaneous (SC) on right upper arm. Administer at completion of 9 months (270 days).
- JE-1 (Japanese Encephalitis): 0.5 ml SC on left upper arm in endemic districts.
- PCV Booster: 0.5 ml IM on anterolateral mid-thigh.
- Vitamin A 1st Dose: 1 ml (100,000 International Units) orally with spoon.

### IMM-1.6: Sixteen to Twenty-Four Months Immunisation
- MR 2nd Dose: 0.5 ml SC on right upper arm.
- DPT Booster 1: 0.5 ml IM on anterolateral mid-thigh.
- OPV Booster: 2 drops orally.
- JE-2: 0.5 ml SC on left upper arm in endemic districts.
- Vitamin A 2nd Dose: 2 ml (200,000 IU) orally; subsequent doses given every 6 months up to 5 years (total 9 doses).

### IMM-1.7: Five to Six Years Immunisation
- DPT Booster 2: 0.5 ml IM in upper arm (deltoid). Strengthens immunity against Diphtheria, Pertussis, and Tetanus.

### IMM-1.8: School Age (Ten and Sixteen Years)
- Td at 10 Years: 0.5 ml IM in upper arm (deltoid).
- Td at 16 Years: 0.5 ml IM in upper arm (deltoid).

### IMM-1.9: Cold Chain and Adverse Events Following Immunisation (AEFI)
- Vaccine Storage: Store all vaccines between +2°C and +8°C in Ice-Lined Refrigerators (ILR) or conditioned vaccine carriers.
- Never freeze freeze-sensitive vaccines (Pentavalent, Td, PCV, Hep B).
- Minor AEFI: Mild fever and injection site swelling are normal; counsel parents to give paracetamol drops/syrup and cold water compress.
- Serious AEFI (Anaphylaxis, persistent scream, seizures): Maintain airway, give injection adrenaline if trained/ANM available, and refer urgently to Chandrapuri District Hospital.
